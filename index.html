What to expect

We’re a remote company. A n8ncation is a chance to collaborate with colleagues you might not usually work with.

  • Unique Chance: Live, work and collaborate together with people from different teams and gain fresh perspectives and creative solutions
  • Epic Work-Life-Balance: Work your regular remote hours, then enjoy unique after-work activities like surfing, beach volleyball or sunset hikes.
  • Inspiring Location: Swap your home office for a vibrant new setting that creates unforgettable memories.

n8ncations are organized by n8n employees, for n8n employees. They aren’t sponsored by n8n directly, at least not yet 😉
